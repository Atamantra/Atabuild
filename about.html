Our story

We built AtaBuild for the buyer we kept meeting.

Through Ata Travel, we have spent years bringing British travellers to Türkiye's south-west coast. Many of them came back again and again, and eventually asked the same question: could they own a place here?

Those who tried often had a hard time. They heard about deposits paid to developers who stopped answering, deeds that turned out to have problems, and builds that stood half-finished for years. Usually the land and the builders weren't the problem. What was missing was someone accountable, on the buyer's side, who would explain each step in plain English.

AtaBuild is our answer. We are a Turkish construction and consultancy firm backed by a UK-registered parent. We work to a written process from the first plot visit to the years after handover, and you can hold us to every word of it.

What we commit to

Six commitments we put in writing.

These are part of how every AtaBuild contract is written. If we fall short of one, you can point to it.

Title before money

We verify the deed, zoning and clearances before you pay anything towards land.

Pay for finished work

Build payments follow completed, inspected stages. You are never far ahead of the work on site.

Everything in English

Every document you sign comes with an English translation and an explanation of what it does.

No surprise costs

Any change to the specification is priced and approved by you in writing before it is built.

You always know

A weekly report with photos and video, whether or not there is news. Silence from site is never acceptable.

Independent advice welcome

Bring your own lawyer, surveyor or adviser at any stage. We will share everything they ask for.
