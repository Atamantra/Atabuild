The AtaBuild process

From a raw plot to a finished home, in eight managed stages.

One team carries your project from the first conversation to the years after handover. You always know which stage you are in, what it costs, and what comes next.

01 Plan

Property & investment consultancy

We start with what you want from the house: a retirement home, a family holiday base, or a rental investment. Then we show you where it makes sense, with honest figures on land prices, build costs, rental yields and resale in each area.

02 Plan

Land acquisition & topography

We source plots on and off the market, then survey them properly: topographic mapping, soil and geotechnical tests, access, utilities and the zoning that decides how much you can build. Plots that fail are dropped before you spend money on them.

Legal & permit navigation

We handle Turkish municipality permits, the Tapu transfer at the Land Registry, foreign-buyer and military-zone clearances, and the building licence. Your agreement with us is made through our UK framework, and every document is translated into English before you sign.

  • Approved building permit (yapı ruhsatı)

Bespoke architectural design

Our architects design the villa around your plot, your views and the way you live. You walk through 3D models and floor plans on screen, and we refine them with you until the drawings are signed off for permit and construction.

You receive

05 Build

Full-scale construction & engineering

Groundworks, reinforced-concrete frame to current Turkish seismic code, masonry, roofing, plumbing and electrics. We specify the materials, inspect every stage, and nothing is concealed until it has been checked and photographed.

Questions UK buyers ask

Straight answers before you commit.

Can a UK citizen buy land in Türkiye?

Yes. British citizens can buy freehold land and property in their own name, subject to standard checks such as military and security-zone clearance and area limits. We confirm a plot qualifies before you make any offer.

Do I need to be in Türkiye during the build?

No. Many of our clients visit for the plot viewing and the handover only. With a power of attorney and our weekly reports, the rest can be managed from the UK.

How are payments protected?

Your build is priced against a fixed specification and paid in stages. Each payment follows a finished, inspected and photographed stage, so you are never paying far ahead of the work on site.

How long does a custom villa take?

Typically 14 to 18 months from first consultation to keys, depending on the plot and permits. Your programme is set in writing once the design is signed off.

What does the first consultation cost?

Nothing. It is a one-hour video call with a consultant from our UK office to talk through your goals, budget and preferred areas. There is no obligation to proceed.

Can you manage the house after it is built?

Yes. We offer maintenance, pool and garden care, key-holding and holiday-rental management, so the house is cared for while you are in the UK.
