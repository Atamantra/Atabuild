01

Plan

Property & investment consultancy

Timing
Weeks 1–3
Led by
UK client consultant

Before anyone talks about plots or floor plans, we work out what the house is for. A home you retire to, a summer base for the family and a rental investment each point to different towns, different plots and a different design.

What's included

  • Goals and budget session by video, from our UK office
  • Area comparison across Bodrum, Fethiye, Kalkan, Kaş and Antalya
  • Land and build cost benchmarks for each area
  • Rental model: nightly rates, season length and occupancy
  • Viewing-trip planning, with plots shortlisted before you fly

You receive

  • Written area report and plot shortlist
  • Total project budget in sterling, from land to furniture
  • Return and running-cost model for rental projects
  • Viewing-trip itinerary

Your part: tell us how you want to use the house and what you want to spend. For tax and currency planning, we work alongside your own UK adviser rather than replacing them.

02

Plan

Land acquisition & topography

Timing
Months 1–3
Led by
Land team & surveyor

On the Turkish coast, the plot decides almost everything: what you can build, what it costs to build, and what it will be worth. We source land on and off the market and test each plot before we recommend it.

What's included

  • Plot sourcing through our local network, including off-market land
  • Zoning check: permitted floor area, footprint and height
  • Topographic survey of slope, levels and boundaries
  • Geotechnical tests, with boreholes where the ground needs it
  • Access, road frontage, water, power and drainage checks
  • Price negotiation on your behalf

You receive

  • Due-diligence pack for each shortlisted plot
  • A clear go or no-go recommendation, with reasons
  • Buildable-area study showing the villa you could build

Terms you'll hear

İmar durumu
The municipality's zoning certificate for a plot. It sets what can be built.
Emsal (KAKS)
Floor-area ratio. An emsal of 0.20 on 1,000 m² allows about 200 m² of floor space.
TAKS
Footprint ratio: how much of the plot the building may cover.
Halihazır harita
The existing-conditions topographic map of the plot.
Zemin etüdü
The geotechnical (soil) report the structural engineer designs from.
03

Secure

Legal & permit navigation

Timing
Months 2–5
Led by
Legal coordinator

Buying land in Türkiye as a foreign national follows a defined process at the Land Registry, but it has more steps than buying in the UK. We run each step, translate every document into English, and tell you plainly what you are signing and why.

What's included

  • Turkish tax number and bank account set-up
  • Foreign-buyer application and military-zone clearance
  • Licensed valuation report, required for foreign buyers
  • Title deed transfer at the Land Registry, with a sworn translator
  • Power of attorney at a notary, if you'd rather not travel
  • Building permit application with the municipality

You receive

  • Title deed (Tapu) registered in your name
  • Approved building permit
  • English translations of every document you sign

Terms you'll hear

Tapu
The title deed, issued by the Land Registry and Cadastre office.
Değerleme raporu
An official valuation report. Foreign buyers need one before transfer.
Vekaletname
A notarised power of attorney that lets a representative act for you.
Yapı ruhsatı
The building permit. No construction starts without it.

Your part: passport copies, a few signatures and, if you wish, one notary visit. You are always free to appoint your own independent lawyer, and we will work with them.

04

Design

Bespoke architectural design

Timing
Months 2–4
Led by
Project architect

Every AtaBuild home is designed for its own plot. We orient the house to the view and the sun, work with the slope rather than against it, and plan rooms around the way you will actually live there, in August and in January.

What's included

  • Design brief and concept options
  • 3D model and rendered views you can walk through on screen
  • Two full rounds of revisions on layout and elevations
  • Shading, cross-ventilation and insulation planned from day one
  • Architectural, structural, mechanical and electrical drawings for the permit

You receive

  • Signed-off floor plans and 3D visuals
  • Full permit and construction drawing set
  • Specification and fixed build price

Your part: review sessions by video, usually three or four over the design stage. The design is not final until you sign it off.

05

Build

Full-scale construction & engineering

Timing
Months 5–14
Led by
Site engineer

This is where most overseas builds go wrong, so it is where we are strictest. The structure is engineered to Türkiye's current earthquake code, checked by an independent licensed inspection firm, and photographed before anything is covered up.

What's included

  • Site set-up, excavation and retaining walls for sloping plots
  • Foundations and reinforced-concrete frame, with concrete test samples
  • Waterproofing, masonry and roof
  • Plumbing, drainage, electrics and air conditioning
  • Stone cladding, render and external joinery

You receive

  • Stage sign-off before each payment is due
  • Inspection records and concrete test results
  • Photo record of every concealed element

Terms you'll hear

TBDY 2018
The Turkish Building Earthquake Code that governs structural design.
Yapı denetim
A licensed, independent building-inspection firm. Its oversight is a legal requirement.
Hakediş
A progress payment against completed, measured work.
06

Build · throughout

Dedicated project management

Timing
Day one to handover
Led by
Your project manager

You get one project manager who speaks English, knows your build in detail and answers to you. Their job is to make the site as visible from the UK as it would be if you lived next door.

What's included

  • Weekly written report with photos and a short video walk-round
  • Monthly video call to review programme and budget
  • Shared project folder with drawings, permits and invoices
  • Any change priced and approved by you in writing before work starts
  • Site visits arranged around your trips

You receive

  • A named contact for the whole project
  • Live programme against the contract dates
  • Budget tracker with every payment and change

Your part: read the weekly report and make decisions when we ask. We tell you well in advance when a choice is coming.

07

Finish

Interior fit-out & finishing

Timing
Months 12–16
Led by
Interiors lead

The finishing stage turns a structure into your home. We make joinery to measure in local workshops, source stone and timber from trusted suppliers, and plan the garden for a dry Mediterranean summer.

What's included

  • Made-to-measure kitchen, wardrobes and joinery
  • Travertine, marble, ceramic or oak flooring
  • Bathrooms, sanitaryware and lighting
  • Smart-home controls for lighting, climate, security and cameras
  • Pool, terraces, drought-tolerant planting and irrigation
  • Optional furniture and soft-furnishing package

You receive

  • Finishes schedule with samples you approve
  • Smart-home set-up you can control from the UK
  • Landscaped, ready-to-live-in home

Your part: choose finishes in our showroom on a visit, or from samples sent to you and reviewed together by video.

08

Finish · and after

Turnkey handover & management

Timing
Month 15 onward
Led by
Aftercare team

Handover is a process, not a handshake. We snag the house with you, obtain the occupancy permit, connect the utilities in your name and give you a complete file. After that, we can look after the house for as long as you like.

Handover

  • Joint snagging inspection, with every item fixed and signed off
  • Occupancy permit (iskan) and utility connections in your name
  • Compulsory earthquake insurance (DASK) arranged
  • Handover file: as-built drawings, warranties and manuals

Aftercare, optional

  • Key-holding and regular property checks
  • Pool, garden and maintenance plans
  • Holiday-rental management and guest changeovers
  • Annual condition report

Terms you'll hear

İskan
The occupancy permit confirming the house was built to its approved plans.
DASK
Türkiye's compulsory earthquake insurance for residential buildings.
